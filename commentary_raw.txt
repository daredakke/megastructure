# About the project

I wanted to make some kind of diorama, sort of like the drainage thing I put together two years ago. I toyed with a few ideas before settling on the art gallery from SSR chapter 35. I figured it'd be nice to have a space you could move through to view つくみず's art instead of just a file explorer. Plus it meant I didn't have to do so much 3D modelling as it can get rather dull.

There are 355 images in this gallery split between several collections. Some pictures easily belong to multiple collections and I've not thought too hard about where to put things, so forgive me if you see a picture and wonder why it is where it is.


# Picture frames

All the frames are procedurally generated in editor and attached on game launch.


# Reverb

There is a simple dynamic reverb system that tries to make moving between exterior and interior spaces a bit more convincing. Your mileage may vary.


# Environment

The environment beyond the art gallery is a bit sparse. This reflects how things are in chapter 35 and is one of the reasons why I went with this idea over, say, the pipe maze before the factory that Ishii points the girls to. I got a bit lazy with this part and my official justification is that the art gallery is the primary focus, though I was really just worn out.
